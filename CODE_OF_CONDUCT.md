# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone.  
All contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to uphold the standards described below.

---

## 1. Our Standards

We strive to create a community where everyone:

- Is treated with respect and kindness
- Communicates clearly and constructively
- Collaborates in good faith
- Welcomes diverse perspectives and experiences
- Focuses on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Offering thoughtful feedback
- Helping others understand the codebase
- Being patient with newcomers
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Showing appreciation for contributions

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Insults, derogatory comments, or threats
- Disruptive or aggressive communication
- Sharing private information without permission
- Any behavior that makes others feel unsafe or unwelcome

---

## 3. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Reviewing and moderating contributions
- Taking appropriate action when violations occur
- Ensuring fair and consistent enforcement

Maintainers have the right to remove comments, reject contributions, or ban individuals who violate this Code of Conduct.

---

## 4. Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- Contact the maintainers privately at:  
  **support@geekstrident.com** (or your preferred email)

Reports will be handled confidentially and respectfully.

---

## 5. Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- A warning
- Temporary or permanent ban from the repository
- Reversal or removal of contributions
- Escalation to organizational leadership if necessary

Actions will be taken at the discretion of the maintainers.

---

## 6. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions)
- Community channels (chat, email, forums)
- Public interactions related to the project

---

## 7. Acknowledgment

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a positive, collaborative environment.

---

Thank you for helping make this project a welcoming place for everyone.
